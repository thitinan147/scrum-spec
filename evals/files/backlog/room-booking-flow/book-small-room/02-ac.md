## 1.1 Requirement & Acceptance Criteria

**Summary:** AC 2 · EC 1 · Open Questions 0

**approve:** ✅ Alex (PO) · 2026-02-03

### Problem → Outcome

Staff book small rooms themselves, measured by fewer booking requests going through admin

### Business Rules

- **Booking**
  - A room can be booked only in a time slot when it is free

### Out of Scope

- Large meeting room

### Acceptance Criteria

| # | Given | When | Then | Verified by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AC1 | Room 1 is free at 10:00 | Staff book room 1 at 10:00 | They see the booking in their own list | – |
| AC2 | 3 rooms are free | Staff search by date and time | They see all 3 free rooms | – |
| EC1 | Room 1 is already booked at 10:00 | Staff book room 1 at 10:00 | They see a message that this time is already booked | – |
