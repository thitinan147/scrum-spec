## 1.1 Requirement & Acceptance Criteria

**Problem → Outcome:** Users do not know who booked a room → they see the booker's name in every booked time slot of the room schedule

**User Flow**
1. Open the room schedule
2. See the booker's name in each booked time slot

**Business Rules** (validation, permissions, conditions · grouped by topic)
- A name too long for the cell is cut and ends with "..."

**Out of Scope** (include the AC/EC numbers that were cut)
- A way to contact the booker

**Acceptance Criteria**

| # | Given / When / Then | Verified by |
|---|---|---|
| AC1 | Given Sam booked room A 10:00–11:00 When the room schedule is opened Then the 10:00–11:00 slot of room A shows "Sam" | Manual |
| EC1 | Given the booker's name is too long for the cell When the room schedule is opened Then the name is cut and ends with "..." | Manual |

**Open Questions**

| Question | Ask (PO / BE / UX / QA / Team) | Proposed assumption to use for now |
|---|---|---|
| Full name or nickname? | PO | Full name |
